BATTLE OF THE SANDS

Two players enter the desert. Each walks a different path, collecting power from choices made. Then — they fight. Your story becomes your weapon. Your ending becomes your class. The Tarot deals the wild cards.

⚔️ CHOOSE YOUR FIGHTERS

⚔️ HOW IT WORKS

1. EXPLORE — Each player walks Arrakis. 5 choices each. Every choice = a battle card.
2. DRAW — Each player draws 3 Tarot cards from the Major Arcana. Special powers.
3. BATTLE — 5 rounds. Pick a card each round. Highest power wins the clash.
4. CLASS ABILITY — Your ending (Prophet/Wanderer/Ghost/Vessel) gives you ONE special power per battle.

🐍 Snakes grant bonus power. Facing them = reward. Fleeing = penalty.
