🎬 CINEMATIC SEQUENCES

Seven chapters of the Silver Ship's journey over Arrakis

I — THE CRUISE

The Silver Ship glides over the endless dunes of Arrakis. Chrome hull catching twin suns. Sand ripples below. Silence above. ~27 seconds.

II — TURN AROUND

Banking hard at 45°, full 180° arc, then charging back. The ship doesn't retreat — it comes back for you. ~28 seconds.

III — UP & LANDING

From wet sand through rain and lightning, above the clouds to twin suns, then back down through the storm. What goes up... chooses when to come back down. ~37 seconds.

IV — LEAP TO THE END OF TIME

Time warp. Stars age. Galaxies spin and die. The last red dwarf flickers out. Heat death. Absolute void. Then... one impossibly tiny spark. A new Big Bang. The cycle restarts. ~26 seconds.

V — THE BIG BURNING WOUND

The hull cracks open with molten fire. The ship doesn't stop — the wound becomes the engine. Pain transformed into thrust. Preaching is DONE. ~23 seconds.

VI — FEET ON THE GROUND

Fear retreats. The eagle tumbles broken. The tiger cruises. And a 15-year-old boy steps barefoot onto the sand — eternal adolescent, finally grounded. ~18 seconds.

VII — SAFE AND SOUND

We're safe and sound. Finally on the ground. Hearted very so bad. At last I am almost glad. ~21 seconds.
