THE STORY

An Iraqi-Kurdish grocery in Le Mans, France. Not a concept. Not a brand. A cavern.

The spices come from Kurdistan. The recipes come from Mosul. The honey comes from Halabja. The hands that make the baklava know what they're doing because they've been doing it forever.

Ali Baba didn't find gold in his cave. He found something better. He found the ordinary — sumac, cardamom, tea — made extraordinary by the simple fact of being real.
